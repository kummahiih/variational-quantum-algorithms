**Draft abstract:**
We compare two complete coarse-to-fine dyadic orders of finite binary keys and examine their use in a one-layer quantum approximate optimization algorithm (QAOA) parameter scan. For a uniformly positioned contiguous cluster of power-of-two length m >= 2 in a key space of size N = 2^n, the original sieve has an exact mean first-hit rank N(N-m+2)/(2m(N-m+1)). The corresponding integer sum identity is represented by a Lean formalization in the companion repository. In exploratory exact-statevector QAOA experiments on five small MaxCut graphs and three starting conditions, recursive and original sieve orders achieve equal aggregate time-to-target results, while a simple pattern-search baseline achieves higher success by 20 evaluations. The study does not demonstrate a quantum advantage and highlights the limits of extending one-dimensional cluster claims to multidimensional circuit-parameter landscapes.
